Research

Research overview

Our work is organized into five areas. Many projects span more than one area, and most papers are joint work between Center members and their students.

Computer Vision & Signal Processing

Face, speaker and banknote recognition, medical image segmentation and adaptive signal processing, from classical methods to deep networks. The group has released KZ-BD, an annotated dataset of Kazakhstan banknotes.

Researchers: Cemil Turan, Ualikhan Sadyk

Kazakh NLP & Language Models

Language technology for Kazakh and other low-resource languages: semantic similarity, document classification, sentiment analysis, question generation with large language models, and plagiarism detection.

Researchers: Cemil Turan, Andrey Bogdanchikov, Kamila Orynbekova

Reinforcement Learning, Robotics & Optimization

Deep reinforcement learning for vehicle routing under uncertainty, vision-language guided robot navigation, inspection robots, EMG-controlled prostheses and learning-based control.

Researchers: Cemil Turan, Darkhan Zholtayev, Yernar Akhmetbek

Big Data, Distributed & Edge Computing

Scalable infrastructure for AI and data: Apache Spark and MapReduce pipelines, cloud and fog load balancing, IoT systems, and recommender systems built on them.

Researchers: Mohammed Alaa Ala'anzy, Andrey Bogdanchikov, Ualikhan Sadyk, Kamila Orynbekova

Scientific Computing & Numerical Methods

Finite element methods and phase-field models on surfaces, with applications to lipid membranes, validated against laboratory experiments.

Researchers: Yerbol Palzhanov

Papers from all areas are listed on the Publications page.
