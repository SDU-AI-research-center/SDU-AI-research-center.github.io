Artificial intelligence research at SDU University.

The SDU AI Research Center is a group of faculty researchers working on computer vision, Kazakh language technology, reinforcement learning and robotics, distributed computing for AI, and scientific computing. We publish openly, supervise student research and welcome collaborators.
