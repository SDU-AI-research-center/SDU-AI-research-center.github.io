About

About the Center

The SDU AI Research Center is a research group of faculty members at SDU University in Kaskelen, Kazakhstan. We study the foundations and applications of artificial intelligence, with a focus on problems that matter for Kazakhstan and the region.

Mission

Our mission is to do high-quality AI research, to train students as researchers, and to build AI resources for Kazakhstan, such as datasets and models for the Kazakh language.

What we do

  • Research in five areas: Computer Vision & Signal Processing, Kazakh NLP & Language Models, Reinforcement Learning, Robotics & Optimization, Big Data, Distributed & Edge Computing and Scientific Computing & Numerical Methods. See Research.
  • Student research: Center members supervise bachelor's, master's and PhD projects, and many of our papers are written with students.
  • Open resources: we release datasets such as KZ-BD, an annotated dataset of Kazakhstan banknotes.
  • Collaboration with universities in Kazakhstan and abroad, and with industry.
