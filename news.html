  • 2026

    Papers at ICECCO 2026

    Center members presented papers at the 18th International Conference on Electronics, Computer and Computation, including work on LLMs for Kazakh question generation, banknote recognition with vision transformers, suicidal ideation detection with Kazakh-Russian BERT, and robot navigation with deep reinforcement learning.

  • 2026

    Kazakh banknote image dataset published

    A new dataset of Kazakh banknote images by U. Sadyk, M. Yerzhan, C. Turan and H. Wang appeared in Data in Brief.

  • 2026

    Vision-language robot navigation in Sensors

    A hierarchical framework that combines a vision-language model with TD3 reinforcement learning for indoor navigation, led by Y. Akhmetbek with D. Zholtayev and colleagues.
